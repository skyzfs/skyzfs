## Work package

M1.3i: no part of a step is lost in silence.

M1.3h (#25) made the daemon say where a request's time goes. The store's worker lays the parts of a checkpoint's batch (walk, save, apply, home sync, log start, end) among its spans of activity right after whatever made them. The review of #25 removed one of those "takes" (the one after an explicit checkpoint command) and all tests passed: the checkpoint's whole time went under "other", and its parts were dropped at the next request, because a part that lies before the last span's end is skipped without a word. This package makes that class of mistake fail a test. It changes nothing that is measured, nothing in the status file and nothing on the read path.

Commit: `58e1b54..530bd7c` on `zfs-topology`, one commit. Files: `crates/skyzfs-local/src/store.rs` (twelve lines), `crates/skyzfs-local/src/store/tests.rs` (one test).

### Does the assertion hold in every path today

Yes, by reading and by the suites.

By reading: parts are noted in three places only (the walk, the rule's own sync, and the five clocks of a batch), and they are reached from three places in the worker: a request's call (a write that makes room), the explicit checkpoint command, and the rule of the marks after each turn. Each of the three is followed by a take before the worker closes another span, and inside each the parts follow one another in time. The paths asked about:

- *A held attempt* (the log full behind a hold): the take is after the call whether it completed or was put back.
- *A failed volume*: a batch that fails returns early; the parts it noted before are in order, and the take follows as for any step. The failure hook and the hold and position commands note no parts.
- *The hooks' pauses*: they lie inside a part.
- *A step that ends early at a refused deallocation*: as a failed batch, the parts noted so far are in order.
- *The open's replay*: the store notes parts only once it is served (`parts` is `None` before), and the worker's first span begins before that.

By the suites: every test of the workspace passes with the assertion in, among them the conformance suite through a served store with and without marks, the kill and failure tests and the hook tests.

Not covered: the daemon rig builds `skyzfsd` with `--release`, where the assertion is not compiled. So the kernel runs do not exercise it; the unit tests do.

### The test

No marks, so the only batches are the checkpoint's. The checkpoint is asked for without waiting and held 200 ms in the home image's part by the step hook; a read is submitted meanwhile, so the read is the first thing the worker does after the checkpoint. Asserted: the checkpoint took the whole log; the worker's time under each of the six parts equals the step's sums to the nanosecond; the read's causes add up to its wait; the home image's sync is at least 200 ms of it and is the named cause; "other" is no more than the wait less those 200 ms.

With the take after the checkpoint removed:
- with the assertion: this test and two conformance tests through a served store fail (the worker panics at the assertion);
- with the assertion also disabled: this test alone fails (the worker's time under `step_walk` is 0 where the step's sum is 141 ns, and the read's whole wait is under "other"). That is the state the review found, now caught.
