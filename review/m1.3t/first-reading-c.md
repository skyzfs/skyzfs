Notes on rows whose verdict rests on something the author did not name, or on a part that is the log's alone:

- **create_and_open_check_what_is_there:** "second create is Exists" is held only by skyzfsd `create_makes_the_last_part_and_refuses_a_missing_parent_a_file_and_a_volume` (`bins/skyzfsd/src/commands/tests.rs:576-597`). No test in skyzfs-local asserts `OpenError::Exists`; the named `volume::tests::a_second_create_is_refused_and_a_leftover_is_replaced` covers only `Volume::create`, which `inplace::create` reaches after it has already replaced the home image.
- **an_interrupted_creation_leaves_no_log_or_a_whole_one:** the named tests use a halt and a cut without garbage. The failed-sync-plus-garbage variant is held by `ported::a_create_cut_short_leaves_no_volume_or_a_whole_one`.
- **a_failed_sync_is_never_retried:** "the flushed block survives a failed sync and a cut" is held by `ported::a_failure_whose_marker_is_refused_keeps_what_was_flushed`, not by the named tests.
- **a_write_unit_below_the_disks_is_noticed:** the named tests show the refusal and the same-unit exception through the cache. The loss itself is shown by `ported::a_block_larger_than_the_write_unit_can_lose_what_nobody_wrote` with `ANY_BLOCKS`, and the checks by `ported::create_and_open_hold_the_system_to_the_write_unit`.
- **a_write_zeroes_gives_the_home_images_space_back_on_real_files:** the served in-place store writes zeros by direct I/O and frees nothing (decision D6). What remains is held: zeros read back on real files (`the_in_place_store_round_trips_on_real_files`, one block), the store says how (`the_store_says_how_it_applies_write_zeroes`), and `fs.rs::a_deallocated_range_of_a_real_file_reads_as_zeros_and_frees_its_space`.
- **acknowledged_writes_survive_kill_9:** the in-flight write being whole or absent (`tests/store_real.rs:570-573`) is the log's; `acknowledged_writes_survive_kill_9_in_place` says a write in flight may be there block by block. That was already so at the base.

Most doubtful:

1. **nothing_is_acknowledged_behind_a_failed_write.** The author claims "a new instance finds what was acknowledged", but `a_failure_leaves_what_the_volatile_cache_disk_promises` accepts, per block, the flushed content or any version since. The suite says the same ("Acknowledged and not flushed: may or may not be there"), and so does `inplace.rs` point 4. Whether this is a gap or the log's surplus over rule R11 is the lead's call; the weaker promise predates this pull request.
2. **the_store_round_trips_on_real_files.** Both gaps are narrow. The directory is made 0700 by `skyzfsd create`, and the 1 MiB request and the bounds check are held on the simulated disk (suite `rejects_empty_and_oversize`, `rejects_out_of_bounds`). Whether "on real files" and "every file" were the property is a judgement.
3. **a_failure_that_cannot_be_recorded_is_reported_as_such.** PARTLY or LOG-ONLY depending on whether "the failing call says so" counts as contract. At the base nothing outside the log acted on `marker_written`, and the in-place store answers before the marker is attempted.
4. **recovery_validates_what_the_device_holds.** The deleted scenario was a start without acknowledgement after an unwritable marker, with the cut straight after it. The head's `shown_survives` always acknowledges and puts a write and a flush before the cut. I called it HELD because the sync and cache drop in `InPlace::assemble` do not depend on the acknowledgement, but the unacknowledged path has no test of its own.
5. **the_observer_is_told_before_the_marker_is_attempted.** "Nothing more is announced" (`src/log/tests.rs:2537-2545`) ran a later sync and an owner's fail after each kind of failure. The head repeats only `fail_now` after `fail_now`, which is stopped by the `!self.failed` guard before the observer is reached. A store that told the observer again on a later failure, after a sync or write error, would pass.
6. **a_share_of_the_stores_lock_outlives_the_store.** The deleted test ended with a full `Store::open(..).is_ok()` (`tests/store_real.rs:672`); the head ends with `lock(&dir).is_ok()`. A store dropped by a panicking thread that left the volume unopenable would pass.
